Aam Sawaal

Mann Ke Sawaal, Saaf Jawaab

Buzurg mehman khol payenge?

Haan, bilkul. Koi app download nahi karna — bas WhatsApp par aaya link tap karna hai. Akshar bade aur saaf hain, aur design seedha rakha gaya hai taaki har umar ke mehman aaram se padh aur samajh sakein.

Date ya venue badal gaya to?

Koi chinta nahi. Link wahi rahega — hum details update kar denge. Jitne mehmanon ko link gaya hai, sabko khulte hi nayi jaankari dikhegi. Dobara card chhapwane ka kharcha bhi nahi.

Paper card bhi milega?

Signature package mein QR wala paper card design shamil hai. Card par QR scan karke mehman seedha digital invitation tak pahunch jate hain — paper ki shaan bhi, digital ki suvidha bhi.

Kitne din mein ready hoga?

Details milne ke 2–3 din mein design preview taiyaar ho jata hai. Aapki approval aur changes ke baad live link turant mil jata hai.

Mera data private rahega?

Haan. Invitation ka link sirf unhi logon ke paas jayega jise aap bhejenge — wahi khol payenge. Shaadi ke baad aap chahein to hum invitation hata bhi sakte hain.

Payment kaise hoga?

Sabse pehle sirf ₹499 mein apne naam wala Personalized Preview banvayein. Pasand aane par hi package ka order karein — aur wo ₹499 package ki keemat mein adjust ho jayega. Pasand na aaye to package lene ki koi majboori nahi.
